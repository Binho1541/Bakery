Our Bakery Blog

The Sweet Story Behind ChelsBakery
From the first whiff of warm cinnamon rolls in the morning to the last bite of a perfectly frosted cupcake at closing time, ChelsBakery has always been more than just a place to buy baked goods — it’s a little corner of comfort, tradition, and love.

A Humble Beginning
ChelsBakery began as a simple dream in a home kitchen. Chelsea, the heart behind the brand, grew up baking alongside her grandmother every weekend. From flour-dusted counters to laughter over lopsided cakes, her love for baking started early.
What began as holiday cookie boxes for neighbors quickly grew into a full-fledged passion. In 2018, that passion turned into a plan — to open a bakery that felt like home, smelled like childhood, and tasted like joy. Made with Heart (and Real Butter!) At ChelsBakery, everything is made fresh daily with the highest quality ingredients. We believe in real butter, farm-fresh eggs, and taking the time to do things right. Our team arrives before sunrise to knead dough, whip frostings, and hand-decorate each item with care. You can taste the difference in every bite.

More Than Just Pastries ChelsBakery isn’t just about cupcakes and croissants — it’s about community.
Whether you’re grabbing a quick coffee before work, celebrating a birthday, or just stopping by for your favorite chocolate chip cookie, we love being part of your everyday moments. We’ve watched little ones grow up ordering sprinkle donuts, and seen customers become friends — and family.

Thank You for Being Part of Our Story
We wouldn’t be here without your support. Every purchase, smile, and kind word has helped ChelsBakery grow into the warm, welcoming place it is today. We’re excited to keep baking, keep growing, and keep serving you the best way we know how — one sweet treat at a time. Come visit us soon
